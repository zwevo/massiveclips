MASSIVEclips uploader

MASSIVEclips uploader is a private tool used by its owner to publish short video clips to YouTube channels the owner operates. Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is uploaded.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.

▶ Uses YouTube API Services

This app uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and reads statistics for those videos through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
