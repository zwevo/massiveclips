Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

MASSIVEclips uploader ("the app") is a private tool used solely by its owner to upload short video clips to YouTube channels the owner controls. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload videos to the owner's channels and to read statistics (such as views, likes, watch time and, where available, revenue) for videos on those channels.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to publish the owner's videos and show the owner how they perform. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens and statistics are deleted when access is revoked or the app is removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app owner at the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in consent screen.
